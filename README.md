This project explores life expectancy and GDP data using Python and data 
visualisation techniques.

The analysis uses visualisations to investigate differences in life expectancy
between countries and explores relationships between life expectancy and economic
factors.

Objectives
The project explores questions including:
Has life expectancy increased over time in the six nations?
Is there a correlation between GDP and life expecancy of a country?
What is the average life expectancy in these nations?
What is the distribution of that life expectancy?

Tools & Libraries
Python
Jupyter Notebook
Pandas
NumPy
Matplotlib
Seaborn

Project File
life_expectancy_gdp.ipynb - Jupyter Notebook containing the data analysis and
visualisations

Key Visualisations
The notebook uses several visualsation techniques including:
Bar Charts
Histograms
Box plots
Scatter plots
Line charts
These visualisations are used to identify patterns, distributions, differences
between countries and relationships between variables

Conclusion
Overlooking my proceedings and findings we learned that there is a positive correlation between GDP and life expectancy. If a nation has a high life expectancy at birth in years they also tend to have a high GDP which is evident on 5 nations (Germany, USA, China, Mexico and Chile) whereas Zimbabwe has a low life expectancy, it correlates with their low GDP. Another trend that the charts allow us to visualise is the growth over time, as expected, the nations GDP and life expectency does show a steady growth over the years.
